About

I'm an undergraduate at UT Austin studying Computer Science, carrying a 4.00 GPA. I've spent the last three summers and semesters embedded in product teams — most recently at H-E-B, before that Kendra Scott and HEXANIKA — running user research, shipping feature proposals, and translating customer signal into roadmaps that actually move.

I move between product, engineering, and analytics on purpose. To me they're three angles on the same job: figure out what to build next, build it, then measure whether you were right. On the org side, I'm a Product Fellow at Texas Product Catalyst, a Technical PM at the Texas FinTech Collective, and a Senior Associate Consultant at the Texas Energy Consulting Organization.

"Product, analytics, and code — three angles on the same job."

Outside the work I'm an Eagle Scout, a New York Knicks fan against my better judgment, and a perpetually-mid pickleball player. Always up for a coffee.
